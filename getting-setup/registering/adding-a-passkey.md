# Adding a Passkey

We support Passkey login's once you have registered, allowing you to create and save a passkey securely onto each of your devices so that you can login with just a few taps - absolutely brilliant when all you want to do is post your latest creation or photograph or update!&#x20;

To add a passkey onto your account you need to:

1. Visit the account portal here [https://auth.postyfox.com/realms/PostyFox/account/](https://auth.postyfox.com/realms/PostyFox/account/)
2. Login - if you haven't already
3. Click Account Security on the left hand side, and select Signing in
4. Scroll down, and you will see a section titled "Passwordless". This is passkeys!\
   Click the "Set up Passkey" on the right to create a passkey on your current device.\
   Note: You will probably be prompted to enter your password; this is normal!
5. Click Register when prompted for the "Passkey Registration" (you probably don't want to tick the Sign Out from other devices box though!)
6. Enter a name for your passkey (your device is a good name!) so you know what its linked to in the future

All done! You can now login with this passkey in the future.



{% hint style="info" %}
Some password managers, such as Bitwarden, can sync passkeys between your devices. However, if you do not use such a tool you will need to create a passkey for each of your devices in order to make the most of the experience.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Don't panic, there is no limit to how many you can have active; but remember to name them so you can remove them if you ever loose a device!
{% endhint %}
