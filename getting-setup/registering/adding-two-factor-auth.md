# Adding two factor auth

You can register multiple two factor applications with our Keycloak instance on your account; these can be any OTOP supporting client, including Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, Authy and more.<br>

1. Login to the Keycloak Account page [here](https://auth.postyfox.com/realms/PostyFox/account/)
2. Select Account Security on the left, then Signing In
3. Click "Set up authenticator application" on the right and follow the prompts

And that's it!
