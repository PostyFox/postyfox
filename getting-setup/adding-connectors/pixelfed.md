# Pixelfed

The Pixelfed connector authenticates to your account through OAuth; if you encounter any issues connecting to your chosen Pixelfed instance, please contact the instance administrator to ensure that the passport keys are correctly configured on the server.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To get started, enter your instance address and click "Save & connect Pixelfed".

You'll get a popup from your instance, and if you are not already logged in you will need to login; then you will be greeted with an authorisation request like the following:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click Authorize, and you are done!



**Troubleshooting**

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png>)

This popup can indicate two potential conditions:

* Passport keys are invalid on the Pixelfed instance (contact your instance admin)
* You have made a typo
