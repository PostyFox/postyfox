Using the Instagram connector requires that your account is either a Business or Creator account type; if you have a Personal account, it will sadly not work.  This is a restriction imposed by Instagram, not us.

#### Adding the Connector

The Instagram Connector uses OAuth federated connectivity with Instagram themselves ; as such you will not need to share your login details with us.

Click the "Save & connect Instagram" button to start the login flow.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (34).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (33).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
