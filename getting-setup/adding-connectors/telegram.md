# Telegram

Adding a Telegram connector to your account is one of the few connectors which exposes our multi-target capability; where you are able to authenticate once, and target multiple channels or groups.

To add a Telegram connector, you will need to provide your Phone Number that your Telegram account is associated to.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
We \*strongly\* recommend that all Telegram users have 2FA enabled to prevent account abuse and hijack, and fully support 2FA functionality.  We will take your 2FA password during the account addition, and exchange this for a secure code with Telegram allowing us to post on your behalf. A combination of your authorisation to our application (which can be revoked at any time), and this code provide our access.  We never store your password.
{% endhint %}

Be sure to enter your phone number in international format; for example, in the UK the prefix is +44, and you drop the leading zero of the number.

Once done, click Save Connector (it is safe to leave the Default posting target field blank).&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Assuming you have 2FA enabled, click Log In. This will start the authentication process.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (9).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

At this point, Telegram will have sent you a code - enter it and click Continue.&#x20;

You will receive another prompt for your Telegram 2FA Password or Phrase - this will be the same 2FA Password or Phrase that you enter when you re-install Telegram. If you get this wrong, your authentication will fail, however, you can click "Log in" to start the process again.
