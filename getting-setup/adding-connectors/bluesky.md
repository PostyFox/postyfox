# BlueSky

The BlueSky connector authenticates to your account through the use of an "App Password" which you will need to create on your account.&#x20;

You can find the option for this under Settings, Privacy and Security, App passwords in BlueSky.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Once you create the app password in BlueSky, it will only be visible that once; you will want to copy it straight into PostyFox. We do not recommend keeping it saved anywhere to avoid it being compromised.  You should not re-use these app passwords.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Add your BlueSky handle into the box, without the preceding at symbol (@) - you will see a warning if you include it - and your App password into the relevant box.

Ensure that you enable the Connector; if at any point in the future you wish to globally disable this connector without removing it, you can toggle the Enabled option.

When you are done, click Save connector.
