# Mastodon

The Mastodon connector authenticates to your account through OAuth; simply enter the instance URL and click the "Save & connect Mastodon" button then follow the flow.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be prompted to login to your instance, with a screen like the following:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

After logging in, you will need to approve the permissions that are required - click Authorise and you will be returned to PostyFox.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Ensure that you enable the Connector; if at any point in the future you wish to globally disable this connector without removing it, you can toggle the Enabled option.

When you are done, click Save connector.
