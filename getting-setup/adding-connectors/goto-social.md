# GoTo Social

The GoTo Social connector authenticates to your account through OAuth; simply enter the instance URL and click the "Save & connect GoToSocial" button then follow the flow.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (15).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be prompted to login to your instance, which will look something like this.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (16).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

After logging in, you will get this sort of screen - note that the address will not be http://localhost, but **SHOULD BE** https://app.postyfox.com.  If it is anything _OTHER THAN containing_ postyfox.com, do not click Allow on this screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (17).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Ensure that you enable the Connector; if at any point in the future you wish to globally disable this connector without removing it, you can toggle the Enabled option.

When you are done, click Save connector.
