# Friendica

The Friendica connector authenticates to your account through OAuth; simply enter the instance URL and click the "Save & connect Friendica" button then follow the flow.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (11).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once you click "Yes" you will be redirected back to PostyFox, and that is it!

Ensure that you enable the Connector; if at any point in the future you wish to globally disable this connector without removing it, you can toggle the Enabled option.

When you are done, click Save connector.
