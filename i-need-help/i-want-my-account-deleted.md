# I want my account deleted

If you want your account, and any data that we might still hold deleted, please note that this project can only do so for the hosted instances at [https://dev.postyfox.com](https://dev.postyfox.com/) and [https://app.postyfox.com](https://app.postyfox.com/).  Any other instances that people self-host of PostyFox are not under our control, and you will need to contact them directly.

However, if you do wish to proceed with account deletion, please simply email us at **data@postyfox.com** indicating your request, notifying us of the instances (dev / prod) which you have been using and we will action your request.&#x20;
